# Tap-to-Track Decisions

- The app focuses on one daily task: attendance and participation taps.
- A learner card has one large positive action and one smaller redirect action.
- “Start fresh” resets today's counts after confirmation.
- Class rosters and today's counts stay on the current device. This removes the
  need for accounts, a database, or a custom server.
- Only classroom display names are collected. Do not enter student identifiers
  or other sensitive information.
- SvelteKit provides the app structure while keeping the interface in a single,
  approachable page.
